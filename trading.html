Pyrocor Trading builds the aggregation layer that connects fragmented SADC producers to institutional buyers who need consistent supply. In agriculture specifically, the gap between SADC producers and the buyers who need their output is wide, persistent, and largely unaddressed.

The same consolidation model applies across base metals, industrial chemicals, and minerals, wherever technical complexity or geographic fragmentation creates a pricing gap that a well-positioned intermediary can close.
