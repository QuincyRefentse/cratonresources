Independent Mining & Metallurgical Advisory

Craton Resources is an independent advisory firm operating at the intersection of capital markets, commodity trading, and fragmented industry consolidation. We serve clients across the SADC region, providing deep expertise in mining, metallurgy, and commodity finance.

Our practice is built around a single thesis: the most valuable opportunities in Southern African markets sit in the gaps that conventional institutions are structurally unable to access.

With over 20 years of network building and knowledge accumulation, we bridge the gap between fragmented producers and institutional buyers, between borrowers and lenders, and between opportunity and capital.

Our Mission

To unlock value in SADC markets by building the infrastructure that connects fragmented supply to institutional demand.

Our Vision

To be the leading independent advisory firm for mining, metallurgical, and commodity markets in Southern Africa.

Our Integrated Practice

01

Advisory

Originating, structuring, and placing debt for mid-market SADC businesses with liquid assets that banks decline to lend against.

02
