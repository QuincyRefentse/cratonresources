The advisory gap: debt that banks will not build.

Home / Advisory

Mid-market SADC businesses carry liquid assets on their balance sheets that banks decline to lend against. Pyrocor Advisory closes that gap as an independent originating arranger. We originate, structure, document and place. Arrangement fee paid at closing from facility proceeds.

01

Deal Origination

Our commodity network surfaces opportunities that never reach a bank desk. We identify and source deals through our deep relationships across the SADC mining and agricultural sectors.

02

Facility Structuring

Built from the asset up, into bankable structures lenders can approve. We design financing solutions that work for both borrowers and lenders.

03

Capital Placement

Non-bank lenders, family offices and DFIs. We place what banks decline, connecting borrowers with the right capital partners.

04

Asset-Backed Structuring

Commodity stocks and receivables priced from the market up. We structure facilities backed by physical assets that traditional lenders struggle to value.
